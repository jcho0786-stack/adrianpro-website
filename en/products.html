Laminate Tubes

We produce ABL/PBL laminate tubes. ABL tubes suit functional cosmetics and pharmaceutical topicals that need strong barrier protection, while PBL tubes offer excellent appearance and shape recovery. Diameter and length are designed to fit your product.

01 — ABL Tube

ABL Tubes
Aluminium Barrier Laminate

A thin aluminium foil in the middle layer blocks oxygen, moisture and UV. The best structure for products whose active ingredients must be protected from oxidation and degradation.

  • High barrier — blocks oxygen, moisture and light to keep ingredients stable throughout shelf life
  • Dead-fold — the tube holds its squeezed shape, minimising air suck-back
  • Recommended for — functional creams, eye creams, ointments and topicals, toothpaste

02 — PBL Tube

PBL Tubes
Plastic Barrier Laminate

A plastic barrier layer such as EVOH provides barrier protection, while the tube springs back to its original shape and keeps a clean appearance.

  • Premium appearance — clear, pearl and other finishes; keeps its shape during use
  • Balanced barrier — sufficient protection for general cosmetics
  • Recommended for — sun care, cleansing foam, hand cream, body care
