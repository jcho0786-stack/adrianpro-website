FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Send it through our contact page.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on tube diameter, cap specifications and whether a mould already exists. Tell us your specs and quantity, and we'll confirm feasibility and pricing.

How long does sampling take?

It depends on the specification once artwork is final. We'll give you an estimated schedule during consultation.

Should I choose ABL or PBL?

We recommend ABL for oxidation-sensitive ingredients (retinol, vitamin C, PDRN, etc.) and pharmaceutical topicals, and PBL for general cosmetics where appearance and shape recovery matter. Share your formula details and we'll recommend the right structure.

Do you work with overseas brands?

Yes. We support the export process from quotation to shipment.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes. With advance scheduling you can see our production line and quality system in person.
