Privacy Policy

Adrianpro Co., Ltd. (the "Company") discloses this Privacy Policy to protect the personal information of website users and to handle related requests promptly, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.

1. Purpose of processing

Personal information received through the website inquiry form is used only to respond to your inquiry, such as quotations, specification consultations, sample requests and factory visit scheduling.

2. Information we collect

  • Required: company name, contact name, phone number, email
  • Optional: inquiry type, tube diameter, message

The website has no membership function and the Company does not use cookies to identify visitors. The embedded Google Map on our location sections may use cookies under Google's own policies.

3. Retention period

Information is kept for 3 years after the inquiry or business consultation ends and is then deleted without delay. Where a transaction takes place, records may be kept for the period required by applicable laws.

4. Provision to third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

5. Outsourcing and international transfer

To deliver inquiries to our company email, we use the services below. Your information may be transmitted to and stored on servers outside Korea.

6. Destruction

Information is destroyed without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose has been achieved. Electronic files are permanently deleted and paper documents are shredded.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time using the contact details below.

8. Security measures

Access to personal information is limited to the minimum number of staff, account passwords are managed, and inquiries are sent over encrypted connections (https).
