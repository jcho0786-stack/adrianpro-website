Facility & Quality

A fully automated line from tube forming to capping and sealing, vision inspection at every stage, and an MES that captures all the data. Quality that doesn't depend on individual skill.

Production Line

Three processes,
one line

Tube body forming, shoulder injection and joining, and capping and sealing run as one continuous automated process, for tubes from Ø13.7 to 60 mm.

STEP 01

Tube welding & cutting

Laminate film is high-frequency welded into a cylinder and cut to length.

Vision inspection
STEP 02

Shoulder injection & joining

The shoulder is injection-moulded and joined to the tube body in one step.

Vision inspection
STEP 03

Capping & aluminium sealing

Caps are fitted and an aluminium inner seal is applied to finish each tube ready for shipment.

Quality System

The system finds
defects first

In-line vision inspection rejects defects automatically during production, and defect counts and types are logged in the MES for lot-by-lot traceability.

Vision 1 · Tube process

100% camera inspection for print defects, contamination and weld quality.

Vision 2 · Shoulder process

100% inspection for short shots, surface defects and joint weld quality.

QC Flow

  1. Incoming inspection

    Film, cap and resin checked against material standards

  2. First-article check

    Dimensions, print and fit verified at start-up

  3. In-line inspection

    100% vision inspection with automatic rejection

  4. In-process & final

    Lot sampling inspection with records kept

  5. CAPA

    Root-cause analysis and prevention for any nonconformity
